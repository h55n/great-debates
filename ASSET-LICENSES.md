## Hero drawing

A Seated Saint Reading from a Book, attributed to Francesco Botticini. The Metropolitan Museum of Art. CC0 / Open Access.

Source: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/459470
Original image: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/rl/original/DP358993.jpg
reader-ink.png is an editorial tonal and transparency derivative made for this website. It is decorative, not a depiction of a debate participant.

## Painting band

The Creation of Adam, Michelangelo, 1508-1512. Public-domain artwork, PD-old / PD-Art reproduction.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Creation_of_Adam_(1).png
PD-Art describes faithful reproductions of two-dimensional public-domain artwork. Wikimedia notes that photographic reproduction rights can vary by jurisdiction.
